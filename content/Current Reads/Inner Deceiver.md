## Inner deceiver

 It judges you, demeans you, shines light on your weaknesses.
- You must learn to subdue it if you want to get that limitless mindset.


## Common Archetypes of Inner Deceiver

- Classic Judge
	- Call out the judge to psychologically distance from yourself. Speak like a third person.
- Victimizer 
	- Tells you that universe is against you.
	- See it always happen to you.
	- Internal locus of control. You can choose to focus on what you have control over.
	- Cognitive Shifting - This is what is happening. Speak from position of power and choose to take action. 
- Misguided Protector
	- Slow down
	- You're too old, too young. Protects you from judgement and criticism by stopping you from taking action. You're safe but stuck then.
	- You never feel ready because of this
	- Take the action. Make process the goal.
- The RingMaster
	- Productivity guilt
		- You feel guilty whenever you're not busy.
	- It tells you that your worth is directly tied to how productive you are.
	- You immediately jump from one goal to another with no satisfaction.
		-  Unfulfilling treadmill of achievement addiction.
	- No matter how great your achievement is, you'd never be good enough for the ringmaster.
	- You put yourself on the risk of burnout.
- The Neglector
	- If you feel insecure in your work, you constantly seek validation from others, and put their needs above you. 
	- Seek approval behavior. 


> _If you let people's perception of you affect your behavior, you'll never grow as a person.

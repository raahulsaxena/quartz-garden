# Hi, I’m Rahul 👋

Welcome to my Digital Garden — a growing collection of ideas, notes, experiments, and technical insights I'm cultivating as I learn.

This garden isn’t polished or perfect. It’s a living, breathing reflection of my thinking — shaped by what I’m exploring in machine learning, system design, generative AI, and beyond. Some thoughts are seedlings 🌱, others are more developed. All are open to revision.

Here, you might find:
- Technical breakdowns from ML and GenAI research
- Notes on systems thinking, engineering philosophy, or personal workflows
- Raw ideas I’m still working through — and that might change tomorrow

This site is built with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) + [Quartz](https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz) — tools I use to connect thoughts like nodes in a graph rather than steps in a line.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to wander.
